Featured Research Project · 2026

Kahawa Connect (EUDR & Coffee Health App)

Built as Twinamasiko Jusper's final-year research project, Kahawa Connect equips Ugandan smallholder coffee farmers and cooperative field agents to capture GPS farm polygons completely offline, verify deforestation-free status against Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, scan coffee leaves for disease using on-device machine learning, and compare verified district market prices across Fresh Cherries, Kiboko, and FAQ states.

Edge Stack: Flutter, Drift (SQLite), TFLite
Cloud & Geo Stack: Supabase PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Google Earth Engine
STAGE 1 · ANDROID OFFLINE CAPTURE
Flutter · Drift SQLite

Field agent walks coffee plot boundary offline; 6 GPS polygon vertices queued locally in Drift SQLite.
